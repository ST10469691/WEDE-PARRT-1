Our Story

Sisco Solutions and Training was founded by Sisco Ndhlovu, a passionate entrepreneur and baking expert

We combine culinary education, cake artistry, and business consulting to empower bakers and entrepreneurs.

Mission: To empower individuals with baking and entrepreneurial skills while delivering high-quality confectionery experiences.

Vision: To be South Africa’s leading baking school and cake hub, inspiring creativity, entrepreneurship, and excellence.
